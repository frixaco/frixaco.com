Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: completely new personal portfolio homepage concept, one desktop screenshot.
Primary request: Design a bold, beautiful, expressive NEOBRUTALIST PERSONAL EXHIBITION for Rustam / frixaco.com. The previous exploration was an overcrowded typographic collage; this must be a fundamentally different approach with excellent readability and an obvious interaction model. No input reference image: invent a new composition.

Core concept: a website with one large exhibition STAGE and a persistent project-selection rail. The visitor clicks a project name and the entire stage changes its imagery, description and accent color while navigation stays put. Left/right arrow buttons offer the same interaction. The website has visual confidence and personality, but is understandable in three seconds. Show Senmei SELECTED, to introduce a different visual subject from prior LeTUI-centered mockups. This is a personal website, not a media player app, dashboard or slide presentation.

Art direction: contemporary exhibition signage meets independently designed software. Hard graphic blocks, thick black rules, square corners, hard offset shadows used sparingly, strong readable grotesk lettering and precise monospaced metadata. A deep ultramarine outer field gives the page a forceful identity. Warm bone-white content surfaces and a saturated coral-red selected tab supply contrast. A small pale-yellow accent can highlight WIP, but keep the color system disciplined. Let ONE gorgeous project image carry visual richness. Neo-brutalism expressed through structure, scale and decisive color, not visual noise.

Composition: single landscape 16:10 full-bleed WEBSITE viewport. No browser chrome, no device, no caption or annotations outside the site. Tightly composed, content-rich but calm. All letters upright and fully visible. No rotated text, no overlapping words, no cropped identity, no narrow compressed display lettering. Main heading around 60px at a 1600px-wide viewport; body 20px; navigation 18–22px. A person should comfortably read every meaningful line.

Header: a compact bone-white horizontal bar edged with a heavy black rule. At left "frixaco.com"; readable navigation "Projects", "Writing", "About" in the center, Projects selected with a black underline. At right "GitHub ↗", "Email ↗", "Résumé ↗". Below it a compact identity line on the blue field: "RUSTAM" in strong white type, beside "@frixaco / software engineer", and one short line "exploring & building cool stuff". The identity takes less than 12% of the screen height and is NOT a marketing hero.
