Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: a fully remade personal portfolio homepage, one desktop screenshot.
Create a NEW design from scratch. The website must unmistakably be an INFINITE SPATIAL CANVAS and unmistakably NEOBRUTALIST. Its subject is Rustam (@frixaco), a software engineer. The last attempt failed because it looked like a conventional two-column portfolio gallery. Do not make a gallery, landing page, dashboard, slide, or neatly fitted arrangement of project cards.

DESIGN CONCEPT — A LIVING MAP OF THE WORK:
The visitor has panned into a large, interconnected field of projects, technologies and writing. The screenshot is a viewport onto a much larger world, not the entire world squeezed into a screen. Layout is nonlinear and genuinely spatial: objects stagger horizontally and vertically, connector routes travel across the field, and two meaningful routes continue out of view. A small navigator shows the viewport within the larger field. There is no central root tree and no rows/columns of cards. The map connects things ONLY when a real relationship exists.

RENDER:
One high-fidelity landscape 16:10 full-bleed website screenshot. Strong graphic art direction, crisp legible typography. Matte near-black canvas with an extremely subtle sparse registration grid to communicate continuous space. Chalk-white typography, thick chalk-white square-edged rules and unblurred black extrusions. Vermilion orange is used ONLY for the currently selected LeTUI title, its outline and the highlighted relationship to its writing. Inactive projects remain monochrome. Large expressive but readable condensed grotesk headlines, 38–64px; monospace labels 16–20px. Much stronger graphic hierarchy and more spatial energy than a generic node editor. No rounded cards, floating soft shadows, ornamental colored blobs, random stickers or giant decorative initials.

CANVAS LEGIBILITY:
Visibly show viewport clipping at TWO boundaries: only peripheral routes/details are cropped, main labels remain fully readable. Preserve enough open canvas between objects to show they occupy positions in a larger space. Avoid enormous dead space. Thick angular route geometry and typographic mass create energy and beauty. The arrangement has no aligned two-column structure or rectangular card rows. The open LeTUI object, oversized bare project names, small technology junctions and linked article strips create a deliberate hierarchy.

At BOTTOM LEFT a small clear instruction "Drag canvas · select a project". At BOTTOM RIGHT a compact black-and-white MINI-MAP with an orange viewport rectangle, beside square zoom controls "− 85% +" and "Home". The miniature MUST reflect a larger network extending beyond the current viewport. It is functional navigation, not decoration. Place a hand cursor over an empty canvas patch to reinforce panning.

All borders, color, labels, routes and controls have explicit meaning as described. This is a bold, authored personal website with a spatial navigation model, not a generic architecture diagram. No drawing-tool toolbar, fake coordinates, diagrams unrelated to the content, hero section, centered marketing copy, project thumbnail gallery, generic cards, big empty demo rectangles or random ornaments. Legible, forceful, dark, independent, materially different from a conventional portfolio.
